# Taylor Cone Theory

Tags: #theory #taylor_cone

Classical Taylor cone theory is the analytical anchor of the project.

The exterior potential of a perfectly conducting cone in a charge-free gas satisfies:

$$
\nabla^2\phi=0.
$$

Near the apex, a separable spherical solution has form:

$$
\phi(\rho,\vartheta)=A\rho^\nu P_\nu(\cos\vartheta).
$$

Balancing electric pressure and capillary pressure gives:

$$
\nu=\frac12.
$$

The cone surface is equipotential, so:

$$
P_{1/2}(\cos\vartheta_0)=0.
$$

This yields:

$$
\alpha_T\approx49.3^\circ.
$$

The same solution predicts:

$$
E\sim\rho^{-1/2},
$$

which creates the unphysical apex singularity that motivates [[Space Charge Theory]].
