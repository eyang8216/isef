1. Poster revision plan / 一、海报修改计划
Design principle / 设计原则：The poster should work as a judging interface: a non-specialist should understand the
problem, method, strongest evidence, and significance within 30 seconds. It should not read like a compressed paper.
海报应当像一个“评审界面”：非本领域评委在 30 秒内就能理解问题、方法、最强证据和意义，而不是像一篇被压缩的
论文。

Recommended new headline / 建议的新标题

  From Electric Field to Taylor-Cone Geometry A lightweight, verifiable framework for electrospray
  analysis 从电场到泰勒锥几何：一个轻量、可验证的电喷雾分析框架

What to remove, reduce, or move / 删除、压缩或移走的内容
    ●    Remove the separate “Application” material from the opening area; retain only one small application
         image or one sentence explaining why Taylor cones matter. 删除开头单独的“应用”内容；只保留一张小型
         应用图片，或一句话说明泰勒锥为什么重要。
    ●    Reduce the number of small plots and repeated figure captions. Move secondary plots, full parameter
         sweeps, and detailed references to a QR-linked supplement. 减少小图和重复图注；将次要图、完整参数
         扫描和详细参考资料放到二维码链接的补充材料中。
    ●    Remove or visually de-emphasize the long equation block and detailed workflow labels. Keep only the
         governing chain: potential → electric field → Maxwell pressure → capillary pressure → residual. 删除
         或弱化大段公式和过细的流程标签；只保留核心链条：电势 → 电场 → 麦克斯韦压力 → 毛细压力 → 残差。
    ●    Do not present published photographs as newly conducted experiments. Label them as published-
         image analysis or external-data comparison. 不要把公开发表的照片呈现为我们新做的实验；明确标注为
         “公开图像分析”或“外部数据比较”。
    ●    Move the optimizer multi-start result to a secondary result box. It is useful for showing parameter
         ambiguity, but it should not compete visually with the main verification results. 将多起点优化结果移到
         次要结果框；它可以说明参数不可辨识性，但不应在视觉上压过主要验证结果。

2. Basic paper delivery plan / 二、论文提交基本计划
The paper should be more rigorous than the poster, but it should tell the same story. The poster attracts attention; the
paper earns trust. 论文应比海报更加严谨，但必须讲同一个故事。海报负责吸引注意力，论文负责建立可信度。

Recommended paper structure / 建议的论文结构
    ●   Abstract: state the problem, the lightweight framework, the strongest numerical and image-analysis
        evidence, and the exact scope limitation. 摘要：说明问题、轻量化框架、最强的数值和图像分析证据，以
        及明确的研究边界。
    ●   Introduction: explain why linking electric stress to measurable cone shape matters, and why
        transparent reduced-order computation is useful. 引言：解释为什么把电应力与可测量的锥体形状连接起
        来重要，以及透明的降阶计算为什么有价值。
    ●   Model: define the axisymmetric geometry, potential, electric field, Maxwell pressure, capillary
        pressure, residual, and assumptions. 模型：定义轴对称几何、电势、电场、麦克斯韦压力、毛细压力、残
        差和基本假设。
    ●   Numerical method: document the grid, boundary conditions, immersed interface treatment, normal-
        field reconstruction, and parameter study. 数值方法：记录网格、边界条件、浸入式界面处理、法向电场重
        建和参数研究方法。
    ●   Verification: present analytical benchmarks, manufactured-solution convergence, independent FEM
        comparison, and sensitivity studies in a hierarchy. 验证：按层次呈现解析基准、制造解收敛、独立 FEM
        比较和敏感性研究。
    ●   Image analysis: distinguish controlled synthetic qualification from analysis of published
        photographs. 图像分析：明确区分受控合成图像测试和公开照片分析。
    ●   Results and discussion: interpret agreement, angle trends, parameter ambiguity, and the limits of the
        stress-balance interpretation. 结果与讨论：解释结果一致性、锥角趋势、参数不可辨识性，以及应力平衡解
        释的边界。
    ●   Limitations and future work: state that full cone–jet dynamics, unique physical onset prediction, and
        new laboratory validation remain future work. 局限与未来工作：说明完整锥-射流动力学、唯一的物理起始
        电压预测和新的实验室验证仍属于未来工作。

3. The story we plan to tell / 三、我们计划讲述的故事
Ambitious core story / 有野心的核心故事

  Many high-fidelity Taylor-cone and electrospray simulations rely on specialized multiphysics software,
  large computational resources, and workflows that are difficult for students or small laboratories to
  access. We asked whether the most important connection—between electric stress and cone
  geometry—could be expressed in a transparent, lightweight computational framework that runs on an
  ordinary laptop. 许多高保真泰勒锥和电喷雾模拟依赖专业多物理场软件、大量计算资源以及学生或小
  型实验室难以使用的复杂工作流。我们提出的问题是：能否把其中最重要的联系——电应力与锥体几何
  之间的关系——转化为一个透明、轻量、可以在普通笔记本电脑上运行的计算框架？

The project story in four steps / 项目故事的四步结构
    ●   The barrier / 门槛：High-fidelity simulation is powerful but expensive, specialized, and difficult to
        interpret. 高保真模拟虽然强大，但成本高、依赖专门软件，也不容易解释。
    ●   Our response / 我们的回应：We built a lightweight axisymmetric framework that follows the chain
        from potential to field to stress to cone geometry. 我们的回应：我们建立了一个轻量的轴对称框架，将电
        势、电场、电应力和锥体几何连接起来。
    ●   Our discipline / 我们的严谨性：Instead of trusting one attractive plot, we created a verification
        hierarchy using analytical references, manufactured solutions, an independent FEM comparison,
        controlled synthetic images, and published data. 我们的严谨性：我们没有只依赖一张漂亮的图，而是使
        用解析参考、制造解、独立 FEM 比较、受控合成图像和公开数据建立分层验证。
    ●   Our contribution / 我们的贡献：The result is not merely a cheaper simulation; it is an interpretable
        diagnostic framework that reveals both what can be computed reliably and what additional
        measurements are needed. 我们的贡献：成果不只是“更便宜的模拟”，而是一个可解释的诊断框架：它既说
        明哪些量可以可靠计算，也说明还需要哪些额外测量。

Suggested spoken version / 建议的现场讲解版本

  “Taylor cones appear when electric stress overcomes the restoring effect of surface tension. Detailed
  simulations can model this behavior, but they often require specialized software and significant
  computational resources. We developed a lightweight axisymmetric framework that runs on an
  ordinary laptop and connects the electric potential to the field, Maxwell stress, curvature, and
  measurable cone geometry. We then tested the framework rather than assuming it was correct. The
  regular numerical component showed near-second-order convergence, and the finest-grid finite-
  difference results agreed with an independent FEM calculation at sub-percent levels for the key field
  and stress quantities. We also qualified the image-measurement pipeline on controlled synthetic
  profiles and compared published ethanol images across voltage. Our claim is not that we have
  replaced full multiphysics simulation or completed laboratory validation. Our claim is that we created a
  transparent, low-cost computational bridge between field calculations and cone measurements—one
  that can help researchers explore designs, diagnose model behavior, and decide what
  measurements are needed next.” “当电应力超过表面张力的恢复作用时，就会出现泰勒锥。详细模拟
  可以描述这一行为，但通常依赖专业软件和大量计算资源。我们建立了一个可以在普通笔记本电脑上运
  行的轻量轴对称框架，把电势、电场、麦克斯韦应力、曲率和可测量的锥体几何联系起来。 随后我们
  没有直接假设框架正确，而是对它进行了分层测试。规则数值部分表现出接近二阶的收敛；在最细网格
  上，有限差分结果与独立 FEM 计算在关键电场和应力量上达到了亚百分比的一致性。我们还用受控合
  成轮廓检验了图像测量流程，并比较了不同电压下公开发表的乙醇泰勒锥图像。 我们的主张不是已经
  取代了完整多物理场模拟，也不是已经完成实验室验证。我们的主张是：我们建立了一个透明、低成本
  的计算桥梁，把电场计算与锥体测量连接起来，从而帮助研究人员探索设计、诊断模型行为，并判断下
  一步需要哪些测量。”

Important wording guardrail / 重要的表述边界
Say / 可以说：“runs on an ordinary laptop,” “lightweight,” “transparent,” “low-cost,” “sub-percent agreement for the
tested quantities,” and “supports quantitative analysis.” Avoid / 避免说：“replaces COMSOL,” “fully solves
electrospray physics,” “predicts the true onset voltage,” “experimentally validated,” or “proves the physical cone
shape.”
