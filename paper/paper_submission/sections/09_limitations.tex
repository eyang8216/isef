\section{Limitations}

The model is static, axisymmetric, and reduced-order. It does not solve transient fluid motion, leaky-dielectric surface-charge conservation, emission-current laws, breakup, plume dynamics, or thruster performance. The solver additionally implements phenomenological space-charge closures for studying Poisson shielding trends; these are not predictive emission models and are not used in the charge-free angle comparison.

The exponent crossing has a deliberately narrow interpretation. It is stable only in the tested configurations that impose the analytical Taylor far-field condition, and thus checks consistency with that imposed ideal limit rather than predicting a physical cone angle independently. In the grounded-boundary configuration, the fitted exponent does not reach $-1$ and no crossing angle exists. The residual diagnostic remains available, but its shallow minimum and initialization sensitivity prevent it from uniquely identifying a geometry in the tested restricted family.

Evidence availability limits traceability. The regular manufactured test, curved-boundary refinement, multistart study, and synthetic-image test can be reproduced from the solver scripts. The FEM source and mesh for the retained fixed-cone comparison are absent. The published-photo workflow cites its source study, but its reduced images, contours, and analysis intermediates are absent. These absences prevent regeneration; they do not show that the retained values are wrong.

Synthetic silhouettes assess a prescribed software round trip, not uncertainty on recorded images. The published-photo analysis is therefore a secondary image and stress application, not physical validation. No new physical testing is included or planned in this submission.
