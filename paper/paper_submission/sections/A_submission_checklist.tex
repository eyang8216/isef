\section{Submission Checklist}

Before final submission, the following items should be completed:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Confirm official Hong Kong selection-fair formatting requirements.
    \item Replace placeholder author/school information.
    \item Confirm word/page limits and adjust length if needed.
    \item Add final experimental figures and tables after approved data collection.
    \item Add uncertainty estimates and repeated-trial statistics.
    \item Confirm that all safety approvals are documented before experimental work.
    \item Clean repository paths and ensure reproducible commands are correct.
    \item Verify all citations and bibliography formatting.
    \item Compile final PDF and check figures, tables, hyperlinks, and equation numbering.
\end{itemize}
