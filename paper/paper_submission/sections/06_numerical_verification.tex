\section{Numerical Evidence and Outstanding Verification}
The local suite currently passes 82 tests. This is regression evidence, not proof of complete model validity. A clean-environment reproduction remains pending.
\subsection{Regular manufactured Poisson problem}
The source-derived quartic test uses $\phi=r^4+z^4+r^2z^2$ and the axisymmetric source $18r^2+16z^2$. On grids $17\times19$, $33\times37$, $65\times73$, and $129\times145$, recorded RMS errors were $1.82155502\times10^{-3}$, $4.44529637\times10^{-4}$, $1.09730635\times10^{-4}$, and $2.72549081\times10^{-5}$. Consecutive observed orders were 2.03482, 2.01831, and 2.00938. These results support approximately second-order convergence for this regular problem only. The quadratic manufactured case produces roundoff-level errors and is not evidence of a convergence rate.
\subsection{Analytical consistency versus prediction}
The classical Taylor-angle calculation is an analytical consistency reference. Calculations supplied with analytical Taylor outer-boundary data are not independent physical angle predictions. Historical immersed-operator rates, finite-box angle estimates, projected voltages, and exponent-crossing values are withheld from the current evidence set until their producing commands and complete error budgets are checked.
\subsection{Outstanding coupled verification}
Curved-boundary field accuracy, interface-normal gradients, coupled stress residuals, domain size, apex exclusion, sampling windows, optimizer initial guesses, bounds, and independent matched reference comparisons require dedicated campaigns. A regular Poisson convergence result cannot substitute for these levels. Projected voltage denotes a fitted balance amplitude and is not an experimentally established cone-jet onset voltage. No matched-accuracy speed advantage is claimed.
\subsection{Image qualification}
The historical synthetic accuracy table is withdrawn: its provenance did not establish end-to-end image measurement. A diagnostic rerun of the current extraction routine failed its nominal angle/profile criteria, including at zero added noise. Those diagnostic runs were not a seeded held-out qualification campaign. Image accuracy remains unqualified; the subsequent image-analysis section describes proposed methods, not completed experimental metrology.
