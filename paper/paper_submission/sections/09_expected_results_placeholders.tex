\subsection{Criteria for Interpreting Agreement}

Agreement will not be treated as binary proof. Instead, the project will report quantitative error and compare it with experimental uncertainty and model assumptions. Strong agreement would mean small angle error, low normalized profile RMSE, and correct onset-voltage trends across controlled geometry changes. Disagreement will be analyzed to identify whether the dominant cause is experimental uncertainty, finite-grid geometry, simplified space-charge closure, or missing physics.
