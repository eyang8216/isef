## Safety and Compliance Framing

The paper explicitly states that the physical experiment will not begin until appropriate school approval, local scientific review, risk assessment, and qualified adult supervision are secured. It frames the physical component as supervised microfluidic Taylor-cone observation, not as a propulsion demonstration or public live high-voltage demonstration.

## Important Framing Decisions

The paper deliberately avoids overclaiming. It describes the project as:

> a reduced-order axisymmetric electrostatic-capillary onset model with effective space-charge closures.

It explicitly states that the solver is not:

- a full transient Navier-Stokes CFD model;
- a full Taylor-Melcher leaky-dielectric solver;
- a droplet breakup simulator;
- a complete emission-current model;
- a plume model;
- a full thruster-performance simulator.
