# Space Charge Theory

Tags: #theory #space_charge

The ideal Taylor cone has an apex singularity:

$$
E\sim\rho^{-1/2}.
$$

A space-charge cloud can shield the apex field. The gas-domain equation becomes:

$$
\nabla^2\phi=-\frac{\rho_e}{\varepsilon_0}.
$$

Model hierarchy:

1. prescribed Gaussian charge cloud,
2. threshold-activated effective charge,
3. later drift-diffusion-Poisson transport,
4. later current-constrained shielding.

The first implementation should use models 1 and 2.
