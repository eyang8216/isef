# Experiment Later

Tags: #experiment_later #safety

This note intentionally keeps experimental work separate from the current theory/computation phase.

Current rule:

> No physical high-voltage, ethanol, or electrospray testbed work until school supervision and required approvals are in place.

Later validation targets:

- observed cone half-angle,
- onset voltage,
- cone boundary RMSE from computer vision,
- possibly current if safely measured by approved equipment.

The solver should be useful before experiments by providing parameter sweeps and predicted trends.
